# Built-in image generation prompts

## v1 — material and silhouette direction

Create a polished art-director review sheet for a complete foreground-sprite redesign of an original side-scrolling space shooter. This is a NEW CONCEPT SHEET, not a game screenshot and not an engine-ready sprite atlas. Landscape composition, 1536 x 1024 if possible.

Art direction: the finest hand-crafted Super Famicom / SNES 16-bit shooter pixel art: elegant readable silhouettes, confident pixel clusters, carefully stepped curves, selective dark navy outlines, restrained dithering, 4-tone hue-shifted material ramps, small controlled specular highlights. Every mark should look deliberately placed on a consistent square pixel grid. No airbrush, smooth gradients, bloom, lens flares, 3D render, vector outlines, painterly texture, AI noise, or excessively tiny greebles. Give ships convincing volume through a few clearly separated armor planes. Make the designs original, not copies of any existing game's sprites. Consistent upper-left lighting throughout.

Neutral very dark navy review-sheet backdrop only; do not design any game backgrounds or scenery. Plenty of breathing room between sprites. Organized into 3 horizontal zones with very small simple labels only: "FLIGHT", "ENEMIES", "BOSS / FX". No decorative border and no paragraphs.

Top zone: the SAME beautiful compact hero starfighter in three side-view poses, facing RIGHT: level flight, subtly banking up, subtly banking down. Pearl-white and cobalt-blue armor, deep blue-purple shadows, a small cyan cockpit, burnt-orange twin thruster flames. Strong tapered nose, purposeful wing silhouette, clearly visible central body. Keep its central body and muzzle in the same relative position across poses; banking changes visible armor planes, not a generic rotation of the whole image. Each pose at equal size.

Middle zone: four enemy archetypes facing LEFT, visibly smaller than the hero: a squat crimson armored drone with a single amber sensor; a long narrow vermilion interceptor with swept fins; a purple-and-bone biomechanical beetle with two deliberate green vents; an anchored gunmetal turret with a clearly articulated barrel. They must remain unmistakably different as silhouettes. Draw them as game sprites, not character illustrations.

Bottom zone: a large spectacular original mechanical fortress guardian facing LEFT, about three times the hero's width: ivory and weathered teal armor over dark articulated machinery, an orange recessed core, two clearly separated destructible turret pods, chunky purposeful masses, bright edge highlights, dark recesses. Beside it, one small ivory/cyan capsule pickup, distinct cyan player projectiles and magenta enemy projectiles, and a 5-step pixel explosion sequence progressing from compact white-yellow ignition to orange starburst, red-orange expanding lobes, dark-red embers, sparse smoke. Explosion frames have controlled silhouettes and no soft transparency glow.

The overall impression must be lush and refined classic 16-bit pixel craft, not cute mobile-game icons, not flat 8-bit blocks and not high-resolution illustration merely pixelated afterwards. Pixel edges crisp. This sheet is for judging cohesion, silhouette, materials and animation intent.

## v2 — native-pixel discipline revision

Refine the attached art-direction sheet in ONE targeted way: turn its too-fine, high-resolution pixel-like rendering into much more authentic, deliberately designed native-resolution Super Famicom pixel clusters. Keep the same subjects, overall arrangement, palette identities, right-facing hero and left-facing enemies, and armor designs.

Critical pixel discipline: the entire 1536x1024 sheet should look like a 192x128 logical-pixel image enlarged exactly 8 times with nearest-neighbor. Each logical pixel is one solid square color block, the SAME grid size everywhere, with no fine subpixel outlines or single-image-pixel scratches. Design the hero as if constrained to approximately 48x24 native pixels; common enemies about 24x24; the large boss roughly 112x56. These are design constraints for this review image, not printed text. All small panel lines must be simplified into strong contiguous light, middle, shadow masses. Only 4 deliberately hue-shifted tones per material and restrained highlights. Keep the white/cobalt hero beautiful and readable with a clearly separated cyan cockpit, purposeful fins and orange exhaust. Preserve the three banking poses but do not distort the central hull.

The biological enemy should have a coherent purple carapace, bone-colored jaws and two green eye/vent accents, with a few confident clusters instead of tiny mechanical noise. The large fortress boss should retain its broad silhouette, teal/ivory armor, orange core and distinct removable turret shapes while losing most decorative rivets and etched scratches.

Projectile cores and outlines must be solid clusters, no bloom or airbrush. Explosion shapes must use contiguous yellow/orange/red color clusters with distinct silhouettes, no soft glow. Flat uniform dark navy backdrop. Remove sheet titles and any other lettering if they cannot respect the coarse pixel grid. The goal is the refined pixel placement of a premium 1993-1996 console shooter, not simplistic flat icons and not a high-resolution 3D image passed through a pixelation filter. Show craftsmanship through shape and deliberate shading.
